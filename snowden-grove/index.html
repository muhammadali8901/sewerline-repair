Underground Pipe Challenges in Snowden Grove

While many homes in the Snowden Grove community feature modern PVC sewer laterals, local geological factors still present real hazards. Heavy alluvial clay soils throughout Southaven swell during rainy winter seasons and contract during hot summer dry spells. This recurrent shifting can displace pipe joints or create localized sags known as "bellies," where solids and toilet tissue accumulate into stubborn clogs.

When a line becomes slow or backs up, running a high-pressure hydro jetting hose flushes away sediment and restores smooth internal pipe dynamics. If joints have separated or developed fractures, technicians apply structural epoxy liners that seal off leaks and prevent soil infiltration.
