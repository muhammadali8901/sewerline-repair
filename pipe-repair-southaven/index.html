What Does Sewer Pipe Repair Involve?

Pipe repair starts with a video camera inspection that pinpoints the exact location and extent of the damage. If the problem is localized — a cracked joint, one root intrusion point, a short offset section — a spot repair excavates just that segment and replaces it. For longer damaged runs, trenchless pipe bursting pulls a brand-new pipe through the old line, and cured-in-place lining creates a seamless pipe inside it. Full repiping is reserved for lines that have collapsed or deteriorated along most of their length.

Signs Your Southaven Home Needs Pipe Repair

Underground pipe damage rarely announces itself all at once. In Southaven's older subdivisions — where vitrified clay and cast iron laterals installed decades ago are now meeting expansive clay soil and mature tree roots — watch for these warning signs:

  • Multiple slow drains at once — sinks, tubs, and toilets draining slowly together points to the main lateral, not a single fixture.
  • Persistent sewer odors around drains or in the yard, signaling a cracked or separated pipe venting gas.
  • Soggy or sunken patches in the lawn above the sewer line path, even during dry weather.
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures run — air trapped by a partial collapse or belly.

Pipe Repair Options: From Spot Fixes to Full Repiping

1. Targeted Spot Repair

When camera footage shows damage confined to one section — a cracked hub, an offset joint, a single root mass — technicians excavate only that segment, cut out the failed pipe, and install new Schedule 40 PVC with code-compliant couplings. It is the fastest and least invasive fix for localized damage.

2. Trenchless Pipe Bursting

A hardened bursting head is pulled through the old line, fracturing brittle clay or cast iron outward while towing a new seamless HDPE pipe into place. Ideal when the pipe is too deteriorated for lining but you want to avoid open trenching across driveways and landscaping. See our trenchless sewer repair page for no-dig options.

3. Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining

An epoxy-saturated liner is inverted into the host pipe and cured in place, forming a jointless structural pipe inside the old one — excellent for cracked but still-round pipes with root-prone joints.

4. Full Repiping and Replacement

When inspection reveals collapse, severe channeling, or deterioration along most of the lateral, complete sewer line replacement installs a brand-new code-compliant lateral from house to city connection.

Which Pipe Repair Method Fits Your Situation?

  • One bad joint or crack: spot repair — one small excavation, often done in a day.
  • Long cracked run, pipe still round: CIPP lining — no trench, 50+ year design life.
  • Brittle or undersized pipe: pipe bursting — new HDPE pipe, minimal digging.
  • Collapsed or fully deteriorated line: full replacement — new lateral, permitted and inspected.
