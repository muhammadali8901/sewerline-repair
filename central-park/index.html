Resolving Soil Shifting & Bellied Pipes in Central Park

In master-planned communities like Central Park, underground sewer laterals must maintain a consistent downward slope (typically a quarter-inch drop per linear foot) to carry wastewater into the city main. When seasonal rain cycles cause the heavy clay soil to swell and subsequently contract, sections of the bedding gravel can shift, allowing a segment of the pipe to bow downward.

When this happens, solid waste slows down and settles in the belly, leading to chronic drain sluggishness. Technicians use commercial hydro jetting to wash away built-up sludge, and can pinpoint the exact depth and boundaries of the sag using an electronic radio transmitter sonde on the camera head.

Where joints have cracked or separated under tension, specialists determine whether trenchless pipe repair can seal the fissure or if localized spot excavation is needed.
