Why Do Greenbrook Sewer Lines Experience Frequent Issues?

Greenbrook is one of Southaven’s most established residential developments, with many homes constructed between the 1970s and 1990s around Greenbrook Lake Park. Compounding this, DeSoto County’s heavy clay subsoil shifts between wet winters and hot dry summers, causing older pipe segments to crack or sag.
